50 × 100 Plot Plans

Plot Plans (50 × 100)

The 50 by 100 foot plot is the standard building block for residential land in Kenya, and getting the layout right on paper saves costly corrections on the ground. We survey, measure and draft plot plans that are accurate, scaled and ready to lodge with your county office.

  • Accurate boundary and setback layout
  • Council-ready plot plan documentation
  • Subdivision layouts for multi-plot land
